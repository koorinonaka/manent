Text to paste into the English (en-us) Store listing in Partner Center. Adapted from the English copy of `site/index.html`.

## Product name

Manent

## Short description

A pinning screen capture tool for Windows 11. Your capture stays on screen where you took it, at actual size, instead of becoming a file. GIF pins, in-place annotation and Ctrl-to-pick capture included.

## Description

Manent is a pinning screen capture tool: your capture stays where you took it. The capture stays on screen at actual size. Drag to move it, scroll to zoom, and lower the opacity to lay it over what is underneath. Pass clicks to the window below to work in the app behind it.

Record a region, and a GIF pin appears where you stopped. Trim both ends in the film strip, delete the frames you do not need, and press Ctrl+C to copy it as a GIF file.

Nine tools: arrow, line, rectangle, ellipse, text, number badge, pen, highlighter and mosaic. Select and move any mark later, or undo it.

Hold Ctrl, and the window or control under the cursor lights up. Click to capture exactly that. The screen freezes the moment you press the still-capture key, so menus and tooltips stay put.

Use the hotkeys or click the tray icon. Choose Manent as the default app in Windows, and Win+Shift+S opens it too. Right-click an image in File Explorer and choose "Pin with Manent" to pin it.

Copy a still, a GIF or the current GIF frame, or save as PNG or GIF. Scale a GIF down from 100% to 25%. Set a GIF size limit (25 MB down to 1 MB), and Manent shrinks it until it fits.

The interface follows your language, Japanese or English, and your light or dark theme. Pins keep their captured size even when monitors use different scaling.

Manent collects no personal information, and the Store version makes no network connections. The name comes from the Latin saying Verba volant, scripta manent: spoken words fly away, written ones remain.
